Visual Perception for Intelligent Systems

I am a Ph.D. student at Hunan University. My research focuses on multi-object tracking in satellite and UAV imagery by integrating multiple sources of information, including multi-drone observations, multimodal data, and vision–language cues.

Building on this research, I connect algorithm design with system implementation, translating research ideas into working solutions for practical tasks. I can approach problems at the system level and carry a solution from algorithm development through deployment and validation on UAV platforms, mobile phones, and robotic systems.

FMA-Net: Flow-Driven Motion-Aware Network for Multiobject Tracking in Satellite Videos

Weiqing Lu, Bin Sun, Shutao Li, Xiliang Li

FMA-Net captures the subtle motion of small objects in satellite videos. Pixel-level flow estimates guide temporal feature fusion for detection and refine identity association across frames. The work connects motion modeling, visual representation learning, and trajectory consistency in a single tracking framework.

Towards Richer Referring Multi-Object Tracking in Satellite Videos: A Large-Scale Dataset and Baseline

Weiqing Lu, Bin Sun, Fuyan Ma, Leyuan Fang, Xudong Kang, Shutao Li

SatRMOT is a dataset for referring multi-object tracking in satellite videos, with 523 videos, 138k frames, and 19k referring expressions across four object categories. Built on existing videos and tracking annotations, its multi-agent framework combines scene understanding, trajectory-based motion profiling, and contextual description generation. Human verification and correction precede automated linguistic expansion, producing diverse expressions grounded in target quantity, motion, spatial relations, and scene context.

Deep reinforcement learning based moving object grasping

Pengzhan Chen†, Weiqing Lu†

This work combines visual object localization with deep reinforcement learning for grasping moving objects. A modified Soft Actor-Critic policy uses rewards based on relative position and velocity to learn a strategy for approaching, tracking, and grasping moving objects. Decoupling perception from control enables transfer from CoppeliaSim to a physical Baxter robot. Experiments evaluate straight and curved target trajectories in both simulation and the real world.

Occlusion-Aware Transformer with Geometry Priors for Multi-Drone Multi-Object Tracking

Weiqing Lu, Bin Sun, Fuyan Ma, Leyuan Fang, Shutao Li

OGTrack links temporal tracking with identity association across drone views. Reliability-aware query propagation reduces the influence of occlusion and blur, while geometric priors restrict cross-view candidates. Shared query representations connect the two tasks to recover consistent trajectories and identities from synchronized aerial videos.

Under review

Prototypical Fusion with Calibrated Correspondence Learning for Visible-Infrared UAV Detection

Weiqing Lu, Jiaxin Li, Bin Sun, Mingkui Tan, Shutao Li

CCFuse addresses spatial mismatch and uneven information reliability in visible–infrared UAV detection. It calibrates cross-modal correspondence, retrieves complementary prototype responses, and regulates their fusion with reliability cues. Evaluation includes public vehicle benchmarks and a paired dataset of power-distribution equipment from real inspection scenes.

02Engineering Projects

Systems for UAV tracking, AR navigation, and industrial automation.

Robotic Adhesive Dispensing System 2021

An automated system for applying adhesive to multiple workpieces arranged in a fixture. The video shows the dispensing heads moving across the workpiece array and applying adhesive in sequence during operation on site.

Mobile Robotic System for Material Transport and Machine Tending 2022

A mobile robotic system combining a wheeled base and a robotic arm for material transport and machine tending. The demonstration shows the robot moving along the production line and transferring workpieces between onboard fixtures and manufacturing equipment, bringing transport, grasping, and loading and unloading into one workflow.

Mobile AR Navigation System with Lane Perception 2023

A smartphone AR navigation system that detects lane boundaries and overlays route guidance on the road view. The demonstration shows lane cues and directional prompts during driving, bringing visual perception and navigation guidance together in a single interface.

Multimodal UAV Target Tracking with RGB and Infrared 2025

A multimodal UAV system for tracking vehicles using RGB and infrared imagery. The demonstration shows target tracking at close and long range, with the camera views and tracking results displayed in a unified interface.
